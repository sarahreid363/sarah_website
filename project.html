Capstone Project: Professional Services Business Plan

Overview

For my capstone, I developed a full business plan for a professional services firm serving a growing, underserved client market. The plan brings together accounting, advisory, and financial planning expertise to address needs that clients typically have to piece together on their own.

My Role

I built the plan from the ground up, from identifying the market opportunity and defining the firm's services to thinking through pricing, operations, and how the firm would earn client trust. The idea was shaped by what I've seen in my internships working directly with tax and advisory clients.

What I Learned

The project taught me how to turn a real client need into a structured business model, and how much planning goes into making a professional service clear, credible, and valuable to the people it's meant for.
