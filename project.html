Breach Project: Risk Assessment of the Medical Sector (Team Project)

Our team conducted a cybersecurity risk assessment of the healthcare sector using the NIST SP 800-30 risk assessment framework and U.S. Department of Health and Human Services breach data from 2010–2022. Reported breaches rose from 199 in 2010 to 719 in 2022, and hacking/IT incidents were by far the most common threat source, followed by unauthorized access/disclosure and theft. The most frequently targeted locations were desktop computers, email, network servers, and electronic medical records. We studied three major 2024 incidents (the Change Healthcare attack, the Kaiser Foundation Health Plan breach, and the Ascension Health breach) and recommended three controls: employee security awareness training, two-step authentication, and firewalls and network security to protect sensitive data.

Skills:
NIST SP 800-30 risk assessment Excel Power BI IT controls
