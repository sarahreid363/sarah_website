Data Analytics

COVID-19 & Public School Finances (Honors Team Project, ACCT 3025)

As part of a team of four, I analyzed how COVID-19 affected the revenue and spending of U.S. public elementary and secondary schools using U.S. Census education finance data from 2016–2022. Following the IMPACT data analytics model, we cleaned messy government spreadsheets (merged cells, multi-row headers, inconsistent formatting), grouped states into official U.S. regions, and transformed the data in Power BI using custom columns and Text.Replace functions. Our dashboards showed rising state revenue, expenditures, and long-term debt after 2020, with the South receiving the most federal funding and the Northeast carrying the highest cost per student.

Breach Project: Risk Assessment of the Medical Sector (Team Project)

Our team conducted a cybersecurity risk assessment of the healthcare sector using the NIST SP 800-30 risk assessment framework and U.S. Department of Health and Human Services breach data from 2010–2022. Reported breaches rose from 199 in 2010 to 719 in 2022, and hacking/IT incidents were by far the most common threat source, followed by unauthorized access/disclosure and theft. The most frequently targeted locations were desktop computers, email, network servers, and electronic medical records. We studied three major 2024 incidents (the Change Healthcare attack, the Kaiser Foundation Health Plan breach, and the Ascension Health breach) and recommended three controls: employee security awareness training, two-step authentication, and firewalls and network security to protect sensitive data.

Skills:
NIST SP 800-30 risk assessment Excel Power BI IT controls

Systems Understanding Aid (E-SUA): Full Accounting Cycle

I completed the accounting cycle for a simulated wholesale distributor, working through the actual business documents: purchase orders, invoices, receiving reports, checks, and payroll time records. Stepping into the roles of three employees with separated duties, I recorded transactions in special journals and subsidiary ledgers, prepared a bank reconciliation, recorded payroll taxes, made year-end adjusting and closing entries, and produced the financial statements and an accounts receivable aging report. The project showed me how information and internal controls flow from a single source document all the way to the financial statements, which is the foundation of audit work.
